What it actually does

Sixteen tools, each doing real geometry and parsing work on your file. Everything below ships today — nothing here is a roadmap item.

Standards compare & auto-correct

Check a DXF against the built-in FDOT 2026 layer standard, your own JSON standard, or a master-template DXF (its LAYER/LTYPE/STYLE tables and BLOCKS). Tolerance geometry diff against a reference drawing with a pan/zoom overlay, then export a corrected .dxf.

DXF project auditor

Scores CADD-standards compliance and flags real geometry faults — bow-tie self-intersections, zero-length entities, coordinates outside the state-plane envelope — then exports an AutoCAD auto-fix .scr.

Traverse & legal-description QC

Latitude/departure closure, precision ratio, Shoelace area and bow-tie detection, with a Map Check report. Parses metes-and-bounds text, chord-traces the boundary, and exports PNEZD points.

Linework editor & field book

Import a P,N,E,Z,D point file or bearing/distance calls, run the field-data checks, and edit vertices and courses on an SVG canvas. FDOT EFB point/reference naming, P/C curve geometry and the chain-list language included.

Florida State Plane & scale factors

SPCS83 East/West/North projections both directions, grid scale factor, elevation factor, combined scale factor, and ground-to-grid distance reduction.

Batch auditing & reports

Audit a whole submittal package of DXF and LandXML files at once into a compliance matrix, a consolidated fix script, and a master scorecard. Every report across the suite collects in one hub with HTML/Markdown export.

JEA As-Built 2024 standards

Civil 3D drawing builder and reverse-read auditor for JEA utility as-builts: 45 picklist domains, layer and block schema checks, and the 18-inch pipe-crossing vertical clearance rule.

Optional local database

Run the included bridge against a PostgreSQL instance on your own machine to persist projects, templates, submittals, linework sessions and the hash-chained audit log. Entirely optional — the tools work without it.

What this is — and what it isn't

You carry the seal. So here is the part most tools bury.

Read this before you rely on a number

  • The reference data is illustrative and unverified. Layer colours, the 11 IDF-zone coefficients, Manning's n and the pay-item mappings were authored for this build and have not been checked against a current FDOT CADD Manual, Basis of Estimates, or Drainage Manual. Confirm any computed quantity against the authoritative FDOT source before it goes into a design.
  • It is a pre-processor and QA/QC aid, not an authority. Nothing here is an FDOT submittal, an official compliance certificate, or a substitute for your review. The self-check summary is labelled as exactly that and carries no seal or statutory rule citation. 100% of seal authority stays with the licensed PSM or PE.
  • It runs in the browser, which is the point — and the limit. Your drawing is parsed in the page and never uploaded, enforced by the app's CSP. The page does load fonts and icons from public CDNs, so it is not air-gapped and we don't claim it is.
  • Sign-in, licensing and payments in the current build are demo-grade. They are browser-local and can be bypassed from devtools. Real multi-user accounts and enforced entitlements need a server; see the note under Pricing.
